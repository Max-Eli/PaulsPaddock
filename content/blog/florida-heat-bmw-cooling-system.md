Brevard County summers mean months of 90-degree days, heavy humidity and stop-and-go traffic on US-1 and I-95. The cooling system works hard here almost all year, and on a BMW it's one of the systems most worth keeping an eye on.

## What the cooling system does

An engine turns fuel into heat as much as it turns it into power. The cooling system pumps coolant through the engine to carry that heat to the radiator, where airflow and the electric fan release it. A thermostat controls how much coolant flows through the radiator so the engine stays at the right temperature.

Most modern engines are aluminum. It's light and sheds heat well, but it doesn't tolerate overheating. Run one too hot, even briefly, and you risk a warped cylinder head or a failed head gasket.

## The BMW-specific weak points

**Electric water pumps.** Many BMW engines from the mid-2000s onward use an electrically driven water pump instead of a belt-driven one. They're efficient, but they can fail with little warning, and when the pump stops the engine heats up fast. Many owners replace the pump and thermostat together as preventive maintenance once a car has some miles on it.

**Electronically controlled thermostats.** BMW uses thermostats that the engine computer can open on demand. When one sticks, the engine can run too cool or start to overheat, and it often sets a fault code.

**Plastic parts.** Expansion tanks, radiator necks, hose connectors and bleed screws are often plastic. After years of heat cycles they turn brittle and can crack without much warning.

## Warning signs

- A high temperature warning or an "engine hot" message
- A low coolant warning, or needing to top up regularly
- A sweet smell from the engine bay or the vents
- Dried coolant residue around hoses, the radiator or the expansion tank
- The electric fan running at full speed all the time
- AC that gets weak at idle on hot days, since the AC condenser shares the airflow at the front of the car

## If the car starts to overheat

1. Turn the AC off. If you can, set the heater to full hot with the fan on high. It pulls heat out of the engine.
2. Pull over when it's safe and shut the engine off.
3. Don't open the expansion tank while it's hot. The system is under pressure and the coolant can scald you.
4. Call for a [tow](/services/towing-roadside-assistance) instead of driving on. Driving an overheating engine is how a cooling repair becomes an engine repair.

## Keeping it healthy

- Check the coolant level with the engine cold.
- Use the coolant your manufacturer specifies and don't mix types. BMW coolant is blue and is mixed 50/50 with distilled water.
- Fix small leaks early, before they become big ones on a hot day.
- If your car has higher miles and the original water pump, thermostat and expansion tank, consider replacing them before summer or a long road trip.
